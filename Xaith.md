Xaith is the Vynrathi word for a large, predatory lizard that lives in [[Abshynthum Kel]]. They are large enough to be used as mounts, if one could be convinced to not try to eat the potential rider. These lizards are hunted by young [[Abthum Shyn]] who are attempting the rite of passage to adulthood. The word 'Xaith' frequently gets erroneously translated as "dragon".
