Papa Grim is the leader of the [Grimling](Grimlings) goblins. He is the current bearer of the [[Grimling Cog Eye]].

Papa Grim is a lead from the front personality. He has obtainied [LASER](LASER (Long-range Attack System Emitting Radiance) weaponry by way of strategic transfer of equipment to an alternate location (STEAL), and is in the process of reverse engineering it.
