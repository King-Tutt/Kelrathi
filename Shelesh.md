Shelesh, usually known as "Sheli" is a card dealer at The Torch and Rose. Sheli is a slender, athletic male Dark Elf who has green eyes and dark grey skin with his head shaven bald.

Sheli is an information broker. He will discuss information at his card table.

Sheli takes payment for information by customers playing cards and intentionally losing to him. Likewise, he pays out for information by intentionally losing at cards to the person he's paying. He can get information to the appropriate people by asking to play Zero Ante.
