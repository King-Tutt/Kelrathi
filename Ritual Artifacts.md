There is a category of items of unusual power which confer incredible boons upon those attuned to them. These artifacts are not simply lying about in the world, collecting dust in some ancient tomb, waiting to be snapped up by anyone plucky enough to attempt and succeed at reaching them. Instead, each of these artifacts must be created by performing a ritual that involves a mundane item and has very specific criteria. The wielders of these so called Ritual Artifacts are known as Bearers. Those attempting to become a Bearer are called Hopeful. Ritual Artifacts generally lose whatever arcane, divine, or primal connection from which they derive their essence when their Bearer dies. There are exceptions. However, those exceptions still require a particular ritual in order for a new owner to become their Bearer.
