Driven from their home by [[Koremakari]] expansion, [[Grimling]] are [Goblin](Goblins) refugees who rallied under [[Papa Grim]]. They are determined to disrupt the Koremakari. This has led the Grimlings to become raiders and skirmishers conducting asymmetric warfare against the Gnomish Technocracy's outer marches.

The

Grimlings refer to other [[Goblins]] who are dedicated to or follow Maglubiyet as "Oubliette Goblins".
