# Premise
An enterprising merchant in Brivalogn is concerned that one of her Silk Gatherers has not returned from the Silkshade Wood. The merchant would also like for the party to retrieve silk for her, since she suspects her missing [[Silk Gatherer]] is near where the giant spiders habitate.

# Setting
The party starts out in [[Brivalogn]]. From there, they will venture into the [[Silkshade Wood]].
