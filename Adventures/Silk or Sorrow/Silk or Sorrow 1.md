# The City Lies Before You
The party has just entered the city of Brivalogn. They have each already gone through the process of obtaining Resident status and are free to walk the city.

>You have just secured entry to Brivalogn. The cobbled streets of the city stretch out before you.
>
>The hustle and bustle of one of the largest trade cities in the world engulfs you. The people are loud, and space is tight due to the press of the crowd.
>
>To one side, a street vendor hawks something that smells roasted with a hint of paprika. Another calls out to test the quality of some ware you don't quite catch as another voice yells over the last.
>
>Under foot, children scamper between people, making their way through the throng before disappearing into one alley or another.

## Metropolitan Guard
Near the entrance of the city are some Metropolitan Guard. These are the peace keepers and defenders of Brivalogn proper.

The Metropolitan Guard is a source of information about Brivalogn, and has an interest in seeing that people new to the city either have or have some way of seeking gainful employment. The city guard can tell the party about:
- The [[League of Free Companies]]
- A [merchant ](Ferolana) interested in hiring someone for a sensitive proposition.

>A lady in half plate looks up at you. Her hair is cut short, to about the jaw, and she wears a longsword at her hip. The livery on her tabard matches the seal above the city gate. On her face, she wears an expression that is somewhere between kindness and business. 
## If the Party Goes for the League of Free Companies
Do not use the term *"Sensitive Proposition"* again. The *"Sensitive Proposition"* will now be referred to as *"Missing Person Retrieval"*.

# Progression
***Next Steps:*** If the party successfully collects the silk of five spiders without causing significant harm to any of them, [[Ferolana]] will request the party capture two mating pairs.

***Strong Silent Type:*** If a Stealth oriented character is present, as a reward they may be given a measure of silk to be fashioned into armor (same stats as Leather but confers Advantage on Dex(Stealth) checks). Alternatively, the silk can be sold for \<X> gold or bartered with.

***Fancy New Shoes:*** The party may be awarded a measure of silk that would be sufficient for making a pair of Boots of Spiderclimb.
