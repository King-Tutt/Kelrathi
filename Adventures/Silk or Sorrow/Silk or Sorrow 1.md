# Introduction
This is the opening [Silk or Sorrow](Silk or Sorrow Adventure).

## Session Description
The party has just arrived at [[Brivalogn]]. [[Ferolana]] is looking for a group to investigate a missing [[Silk Gatherer]].

## Opening Monologue
> With the promise of power, wealth, and influence comes the rise of ambition. Ambition can bring ascension, but it can also bring a fall.

# The City Lies Before You
The party has just entered the city of Brivalogn. They have each already gone through the process of obtaining Resident status and are free to walk the city.

>You have just secured entry to Brivalogn. The cobbled streets of the city stretch out before you.
>
>The hustle and bustle of one of the largest trade cities in the world engulfs you. The people are loud, and space is tight due to the press of the crowd.
>
>To one side, a street vendor hawks something that smells roasted with a hint of paprika. Another calls out to test the quality of some ware you don't quite catch as another voice yells over the last.
>
>Under foot, children scamper between people, making their way through the throng before disappearing into one alley or another.

## Metropolitan Guard
Near the entrance of the city are some Metropolitan Guard. These are the peace keepers and defenders of Brivalogn proper.

The Metropolitan Guard is a source of information about Brivalogn, and has an interest in seeing that people new to the city either have or have some way of seeking gainful employment. The city guard can tell the party about:
- The [[League of Free Companies]]
- A [merchant ](Ferolana) interested in hiring someone for a sensitive proposition.

>A lady in half plate looks up at you. Her hair is cut short, to about the jaw, and she wears a longsword at her hip. The livery on her tabard matches the seal above the city gate. On her face, she wears an expression that is somewhere between kindness and business. 
## If the Party Goes for the League of Free Companies
Do not use the term *"Sensitive Proposition"* again. The *"Sensitive Proposition"* will now be referred to as *"Missing Person Retrieval"*.

###### The League:
The League of Free Companies has an office of sorts attached to the [[Torch and Rose]] tavern. People can become members of the League without being an affiliated member of one of the free companies.

###### The Torch and Rose:
The Torch and Rose will have all of the people necessary for the group to join the League of Free Companies, find work, get information, etc.

The Job:
There is a single job posting on the job board. It's title is "Missing Person Retrieval". Little information is mentioned on the posting. The payout is 500 gold marks. 
###### Sheli:
[[Shelesh]], usually known as "Sheli" can be found running a card table in the back room. He is known, but never confirmed, to be a reliable source of all sorts of information. He operates in subtlety, and expects the same from those who come to him to deal in information. He will absolutely kick people from his table who display a lack of discretion.

When asked about the *Missing Person Retrieval*, Sheli will direct prospective takers to [[#The Merchant|Ferolana]].

## If the Party Goes for the Sensitive Proposition
###### The Merchant:
[[Ferolana]], a merchant and one of the Burgess class of nobles, is looking for a group of people to go into the Silkshade Wood in order to investigate and hopefully recover a missing silk harvester. She also needs the group to attempt to [[#Collecting Silk:|retrieve silk]] from the large spiders that inhabit the wood. Ferolana will provide [[#Supplies:|supplies]], but deduct them from the total payout if the group does not purchase those supplies up front.

Ferolana has the ambition to attempt cultivating Silkshade spiders. If the group is successful at collecting spider silk, she will be interested in employing them to capture live spiders or retrieving spider eggs.

###### The Conflict:
The collection of spider silk is dangerous, and not regulated by the guilds. However, the prospect of spider cultivation threatens to present a controllable resource, and the guilds will not abide a controllable resource whose production chain is not guild controlled.

###### The Agent :
The guilds have employed the [[Quill and Fang]], one of the underworld organizations of Brivalogn, to prevent Ferolana from successfully setting up a spider farm. This [[Quill and Fang Agent|agent]] is unlikely to confront the group directly, but will take measures to disrupt the group.

###### Collecting Silk:
Spiders must be alive to collect silk from them. A dead spider's spinnerets do not produce silk. The already spun webbing in the forest is not suitable quality for collection.

###### Supplies:
- [[Tranquilizer Dart]]: 1 GP
- Net: 1 GP
- [[Peppermint Oil]]: 1SP
- [Smoke Flask](Throwing Flask): 5 GP
- [Sleep Flask](Throwing Flask): 15 GP

###### The Forest

Some of the webbing strands climb down the tree trunks to the ground. As you travel along, there are also funnel webs and sheet webs littered about the forest floor. Off a little way from your path of travel, you see the head of a now dead olwbear protruding from a swath of web.
###### The Missing Silk Collector
The missing adventurer can be trailed down in the Silkshade Forest, though he has not survived.
- DC 16 Wisdom (Nature) check to find the Silk Collector's body
	- Result 20+: The Silk Collector had already retrieved three spools of raw spider silk.
	- Result 15 - 12: The Silk Collector is found near a [[Silkshade Spider]]. The Silk Collector has 1d2 - 1 spools of raw spider silk. [[#Encounters|encouter]]
	- Result >=11: The Silk Collector is found near a [[Silkshade Large Spider]]. The Silk Collector has no raw spider silk. [[#Encounters|encouter]]
- Other checks can be used in place of Nature.
	- Intelligence (History)
	- Intelligence (Arcana)
	- Intelligence (Investigate)
	- Wisdom (Survival)
###### Collecting Silk
The spiders must be alive, still, in order to collect silk from them. A Silkshade Spider produces 1 bolt of Silk. A Silkshade Large Spider produces 1d4 bolts of Silk.
###### Encounters
The [[Quill and Fang Agent|agent]] will not interfere with the discovery nor recovery of the Silk Collector. The agent will, however, attempt to sabotage attempts to harvest silk from spiders. This can come in the way of dealing a final lethal blow to the spider or in use of poison darts to impose disadvantage (the *Poisoned* condition) on party members. All of this sabotage will be done from hiding, and the agent will prioritize evasion, subversion, and sabotage to direct combat. If the party pursues, the agent will open with its crossbow on a stronger (based on agent's perception) target, then use a [[Tranquilizer Dart]] (Bonus Action) on a weaker (based on agent's perception) target.

If defeated, the agent will have a note on its person with the following text:
```
57 40 32 25 11 35 28 20 27 33 11 22 28 20 19 1 31 30 30 18 38 20 24 8 29 40 29 37 13 41 42 13 55 60 25 37 14 27 17 13 12 14 8 33
```
This encoded message states, "Prevent Ferolana's agents from succeeding."
Rather than a signature, the note has a stylized mark representing a quill pen with a snake entwined around it. This is a mark of the leadership of the Quill and Fang.

# Progression
***Next Steps:*** If the party successfully collects the silk of five spiders without causing significant harm to any of them, [[Ferolana]] will request the party capture two mating pairs.

***Strong Silent Type:*** If a Stealth oriented character is present, as a reward they may be given a measure of silk to be fashioned into armor (same stats as Leather but confers Advantage on Dex(Stealth) checks). Alternatively, the silk can be sold for \<X> gold or bartered with.

***Fancy New Shoes:*** The party may be awarded a measure of silk that would be sufficient for making a pair of Boots of Spiderclimb.
