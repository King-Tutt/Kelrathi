>You see paw prints. Not quite in the way that a normal creature leaves indentations in the earth. These are different. Weird. Wrong. Like they've somehow been anchored in place.

The malice hound is an elusive predator that hunts from the space between. They appear as something between canine and feline, though hairless. The skin of a malice hound is grey and blends in with the misty surroundings of the Ethereal Plane. Frequently, Malice Hounds will follow or be followed by a [[Malice Hunter]], with which malice hounds share a symbiotic relationship.
