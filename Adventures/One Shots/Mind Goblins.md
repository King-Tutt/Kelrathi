## Session Description
Goblins have been abducting travelers on the [[Sunrise Road]] near [[Oaken Cask]]. They show up, strike fast, and depart fast.

# Chapter
An Illithid arcanist has thralled several goblins and is using them to retrieve prey and more thralls. The Illithid is hiding in a cave lair in the Feywild. The goblins in question are displaced from their homeland by the [[Koremakari]]. These particular goblinls are [[Redcaps]]. Another group of displaced goblins, [[Grimlings]], led by [[Papa Grim]], are attempting to deal with the Redcaps, as well, to prevent the already refugeed goblins from having a further stained image among the local populace.
