Wuku are clan leaders of the [[Ootadian]].

"Wuku" literally means "wind", but is declined for the animate noun class. Usually wind is considered a sapient spiritual entity, and this change indicates that the individual in question is physical. The word is cognate to [[Vu]].

A Wuku is usually an hereditary position. The Abotadia trace lineage through the mother. While a Wuku can be a man, his mother is the one who would have conferred the position. A male Wuku will not beget a Wuku, because of how heredity is traced.
