The traveling people are a seafaring people of [[Kelrathi]] that ply the [[Highwind Ocean]]. They employ multihull boats, and trade with many various locations. Among these locations are [[Brivalogn]].

The Abotadia are ethnic cousins to the [[Abthum Shyn]], whom they call "[Abashyn]()". The memory of relation between the two people has led to them being the only culture allowed to trade freely with the Abthum Shyn. The Abthum Shyn call the Abotadia "Orthdjan" or "Avorthdjan"

The Abotadia have exclusive trade rights for [[Chov]] and [[Xav]].

The Ootadian (plural form) are organized into clan groups, each of which is led by a [[Wuku]].
