Redcaps are undoubtedly Maglubiyet [[Goblins]]. The Redcaps were among the bands of [[Goblin]] displaced by the [Koremakari Technocracy](Koremakari). They are distrustful of outsiders, even other Goblins, and are slow to forget a grudge.

**Note:** The Redcaps can present an obstacle to *[[respite]]* if they develop a grudge against the Party or one of the PCs. Since the Redcaps are inclined to pursue those that they hold a grudge against incessantly, grudge targets can inadvertently pose a risk to settlements where they stay by drawing the attention of the Redcaps.
