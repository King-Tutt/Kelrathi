The Myrkwood is a swamp about two weeks' overland travel east from Brivalogn. Myrkwood is home to a noble family, the [[Eldritchstag]] family, led by [[Lord Bruce Eldritchstag]]. Myrkwood has fresh water, brackish, and saltwater portions, as the terrain moves closer to the [[Highwind Ocean]].

The head of the family is [[Lord Bruce Eldritchstag]].
