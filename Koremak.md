Koremak is the capital city of the [Gnomish](Gnome) [[Koremakari]] nation. It is a perfectly circular city with much clockwork and artifice. Koremak is run by the Gnomish [[Technarchs]].

Koremak is interested in resources that are in lands currently occupied by [[Goblins]] and in the [[Underdark]] beneath those lands.
