The Redcap was created by Boggle Redcap. The Redcap is the signature identifying item of the leader of the [[Redcaps]] band of [[Goblins]]. It is a beret stained red with blood. When attuned, the Redcap can only be removed by the Bearer. The Redcap remains attuned to the Bearer whether the Bearer is currently wearing it or not. Once the Bearer dies, the Redcap reverts to a normal, mundane woolen beret stained a deep, nearly black red-brown and has the appearance of being caked in dried blood. Once the Redcap has reverted, it cannot be reused in the Redcap ritual.

***Features***
- You are aware of every grudge held by every predecessor Bearer.
- You are aware of every grudge collectively held by the Redcaps.
- You are aware of the location of each grudge target that is still living.
- You are proficient with Simple and Martial Weapons.
- You are proficient with Light, Medium, and Heavy armor and Shields.
- You gain two Fighting Style Feats.
- You know two additional Weapon Masteries.
- Whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make an additional attack.
- You have a +2 bonus to Constitution.
- You have a +2 bonus to Strength.
- You gain five (5) Hit Dice. This averages out to 22 + 5\*Con Hit Points.
- When you deal damage with a melee weapon attack, you restore a number of Hit Points equal to the amount of damage dealt.
- While in darkness, you are invisible to any creature that relies on Darkvision to see you in that darkness.

***Constraints***
- Obsessive Behavior: You insist that your Redcap followers wear caps of their own, dyed red in the blood of defeated enemies. When the caps of your Redcap followers begin to turn dark, you insist on leading them into combat, in order to refresh their caps' color.
- Grudge Holder: You add to the list of Preexisting Grudges any thing, person, or group who poses a significant threat or challenge to you. You add to the list of Preexisting Grudges any thing, person, or group who you believe has wronged you, no matter how trivial the slight.
- Relentless Pursuit: You are obsessed with eliminating those against which you hold a grudge.
- Preexisting [[Redcap Grudges]]:
	- [Gnomes](Gnome)
	- [Koremakari Technocracy](Koremakari)
	- [Grimlings](Grimlings.md)
- The Bearer takes on the mantle, identity, personality, reputation, and infamy of Boggle Redcap, much like how each successive captain took on the mantle of the Dread Pirate Roberts in The Princess Bride.

***Ritual***
In order to attune to the Redcap, you must have an unbleached, undyed woolen beret. You must defeat the current Bearer in single combat. You must plunge the beret required for this ritual into the blood of the defeated former Bearer, dying it red. You must don the new Redcap and demand fealty. You must offer single combat to any Redcap Goblin who does not take a knee and swear fealty. You must strike down any Redcap Goblin who neither takes a knee nor challenges you to single combat. If the previous Bearer dies before a new Bearer can challenge them, the Hopeful must issue open challenge among the Redcaps and strike down at least one in order to become the Bearer. The remainder of the ritual then uses the struck down Redcap as a surrogate for the previous Bearer.

***Note:*** As the Bearer takes on the mantle, identity, personality, reputation, and infamy of Boggle Redcap, the Redcap and mantle of Boggle Redcap are not unlike a form of lichdom.
