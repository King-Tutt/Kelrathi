The black hand is an underbelly organization in [[Brivalogn]]. It is a clandestine organization that performs espionage, sabotage, retrieval, murder, and other covert and clandestine types of work.

The Black Hand frequently gets employed for operations both inside and outside of Brivalogn. The leadership of the Black Hand permits its members to perform work for or with other organizations, so long as that work does not interfere with the operations or interests of the Black Hand.
