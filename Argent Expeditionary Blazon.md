##### **By Decree of the Argent Tower, High Seat of Vigilant Flame and Silver Oath, Let This Be Recorded in the Rolls of Light and Shadow:**

> **The [[The Argent Expeditionary|Expeditionary]] Track**, charged with bearing forth the will of the Spire into the far reaches of the world, shall bear as its mark:
> 
> _Upon a lozenge sable, a tower argent, masoned sable, its port and windows sable, set upon a sun in splendour or; flanked to either side by two swords upright, argent hilted or, and backed by two spears of like argent, shafted sable, crossed in saltire behind._
> 
> This badge is to be borne openly upon the tabard, cloak, or warplate of all recognized expeditionaries in the service of the Tower, and shall signify both **allegiance to the inner flame** and **readiness to enact its will beyond the cloistered walls**.
> 
> The **upright swords** stand for the visible justice and field judgment granted unto them. The **crossed spears**, veiled behind, mark the long reach and silent readiness of the Order’s advance. The **Tower** is ever the bastion; the **Sun**, its illumination and its gaze.
> 
> Let none bear this badge who has not passed beneath the Vault of Proven Flame, and let all who do be marked as **Wardens of the Light** — or its blade.
