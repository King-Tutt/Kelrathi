You may use an action to douse yourself, another creature, or a 5 foot square with peppermint oil.

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of peppermint oil. Target a creature or surface you can see within 20 feet of yourself. 
If you cannot see, you may still throw at a surface: roll a d4. The flask strikes a surface a number of feet 5 times the result.

Spiders, insects, and other similar creatures within 5 feet of the peppermint oil must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature has the Frightened condition for one minute.
