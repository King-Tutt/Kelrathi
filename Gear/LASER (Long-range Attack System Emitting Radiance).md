The Long-range Attack System Emitting Radiance (LASER) was developed by [[Koremakari]] artificers. It is a ranged weapons platform that emits a ray or rays of *Radiant* energy.

| LASER Rifle        | 1d12 Radiant | Ammunition (Range 150/600; Energy Cell), Heavy, Reload(5 shots), Two-Handed           |   Sap   | 9 lb.  | 100 GP [^1] |
| LASER Pistol       | 1d10         | Ammunition (Range 50/150; Energy Cell), Light, Reload(6 shots)                        |  Nick   | 3 lb.  |    25 GP    |

Burst: Use the Attack action and expend half of the ammunition for a reload of this weapon. You fire a spray of ammunition. Each creature in a 30-foot Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d8 damage of the weapon's given type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. You cannot make Reactions until the start of your next turn. 

[^1]: It is unlikely to find these weapons for purchase to anyone outside of the Koremakari Technocracy unless they are black market items at anywhere from 10x to 20x the original cost.
