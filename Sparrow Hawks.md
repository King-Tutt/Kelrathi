The Sparrow Hawks are one of a few rival organizations that operate in the underbelly of [[Brivalogn]]. They are primarily thieves and smugglers, but have been known to broker information. There are rumors that more lethal or intriguing methods of employ fall within the Sparrow Hawks' scope of operations. Nothing of those rumors has every been definitively confirmed, however.

The Sparrow Hawks are rumored to operate in [[The Old Quarter]].

The primary rivals of the Sparrow Hawks is the [[Chimeric Hand]].

Some members of the Sparrow Hawks may also work for or closely with the [[Black Hand]].
