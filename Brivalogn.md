A city-state and maritime trade power found on the [[Highwind Ocean]].

> "Metropolitan" is the adjective having to do with the city-state, its attribution, and its various functions.

There is a strict socio-political hierarchy. While strict, the socio-political hierarch can be navigated up and down.
	- [[Citizen]]: One can only be a Citizen if they are not earning a living via a trade. Merchant or in some way handling the running of a mercantile company is not considered a trade for this purpose.
	- [[Burgess]]: a Citizen held title of nobility

Brivalogn is run by a magistrate and an electorate council. Each noble house has a seat in the council.

***Prominent People:***
- [[Caran VorVenar]] is a wealthy Burgess who appears to take his duty to the defense of Brivalogn and her people within and without very seriously.
- [[Ferolana]] is a Citizen merchant interested in reducing the liability risk involved in the spider silk trade.
