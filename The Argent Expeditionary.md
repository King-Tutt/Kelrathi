The Argent Expeditionary are the military arm of [[The Argent Spire]]. Once complete with their training an Initiate can be selected for the Expeditionary.

Ranks in the Argent Expeditionary are:
- Field Acolyte: a soldier with no command or leadership responsibility
- Warden: a first line leader. Empowered to make autonomous tactical decisions
- Standard-Bearer: an officer in command of several Wardens and their subordinate Field Acolytes. Serves in leadership and planning roles at the tactical and sometimes strategic level.
- Captain: a veteran military leader in command of several Standard-Bearers and their subordinates. Serves in leadership at the strategic level.
- Marshal of the Argent Vanguard: the chief commander and commandant of the Expeditionary corps.

The [[Argent Expeditionary Blazon|blazon]] of the Argent Expeditionary is similar to the blazon of the Argent Spire: On a lozenge sable, a tower argent masoned sable, port and windows sable, upon a sun in splendour or, flanked by two swords upright argent hilted or, supported by two spears argent shafted sable crossed in saltire.
