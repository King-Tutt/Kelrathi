The Grimling Cog Eye was created by Papa Grim, the first chief of the [[Grimlings]] band of [[Goblins]]. It appears to be a clockwork mechanism of brass, steel, and glass shaped like a palm sized looking glass. When attuned, the Grimling Cog Eye protrudes slightly from the right eye of its Bearer, which it replaces.

If physically removed from the Bearer, the Grimling Cog Eye will retain a partial attunement with the Bearer so long as they live and the Grimling Cog Eye has not been re-attuned. While still partially attuned, the Bearer can re-insert the Grimling Cog Eye into their right eye socket, and resume normal attuned use.

Whether the former Bearer is deceased or living, a Hopeful wishing to attune to the Grimling Cog Eye must perform the full attunement ritual. If the former Bearer is deceased, the Grimling Cog Eye becomes inert but the same object is required for any subsequent attunement rituals for this item.

***Features***
While attuned to the Grimling Cog Eye, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain **Darkvision** out to 60 feet. If you already have **Darkvision**, its range extends by 60 feet.
- You have advantage on all ranged weapon attack rolls.
- You have advantage on all Wis(Perception) and Int(Investigation) checks that rely on sight.
- You have Truesight with a range of 120 feet centered on the Grimling Cog Eye.
- While in darkness, you are invisible to any creature that relies on Darkvision to see you in that darkness.
- You are recognized as the leader of the Grimling band of Goblins.

***Constraints***
You will lose attunement to the Grimling Cog Eye if:
- You intentionally harm another Grimling.
- You act against the continued interests of the Grimling band.
- You fail to continue to lead the Grimlings in resistance against the expansion of the [Koremakari Technocracy](Koremakari) so long as that endeavor is relevant.

***Ritual***
In order to attune to the Grimling Cog Eye, you must present the Grimling Cog Eye before a quorum[1](#^f145b6) of Grimlings. In front of the Grimlings present, you take an oath to faithfully and to the best of your ability protect and defend the Grimlings. You must gouge out your own right eye, if you still have it, and implant the Grimling Cog Eye into the empty socket.
