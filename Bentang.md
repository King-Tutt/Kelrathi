Bentang is an empire in the far west of [[Kelrathi]] peopled primarily by [[Elves]]. It is an arcanocracy headed by fourteen noble families. Each noble family sits the throne of Bentang for a century, then steps down for the next noble house in sequence.

Aside from the fourteen noble houses that are the imperial seats of Bentang, there are twenty-one lesser houses.

It is illegal to be a mage in Bentang and not belong to a magic order. This offense is punishable by death.
