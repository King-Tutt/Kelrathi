The Torch and Rose is a public house that serves drink and food. There are a few rooms with beds upstairs, and two rooms for games in the back. At the street level, there is cafe seating, where small plates and hot drinks of dark, strong liquid are served.

This tavern is middle of the road in class. It is the type of place that working class and merchant alike might be found. If a noble were to patronize the Torch and Rose, they would do so with their hood up.

There is a job postings board with work available to any member of the [[League of Free Companies]]. A small kiosk that serves as a front from the League can be found not far from the bar.

Those who know come here to get and to give information. This is largely handled via Thieves' Cant at the card tables in the back of the house. Sometimes, though, this might also be handled at the bar or a corner table in the front.

Jobs of the type that are discrete in nature may be offered and taken here. Getting jobs handled with a degree of separation is largely what a disguised noble might be found at the Torch and Rose doing. Though, none of the higher class clientel would ever admit it, and all of the ones that would be so inclined never speak of it.
