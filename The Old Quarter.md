The Old Quarter is a part of the city of [[Brivalogn]] on the west bank of the river Drea. It has narrow, meandering streets. The Old Quarter is a lower income area of poor and working class with a somewhat gritty reputation. Most street smart people keep their hands on their coin purse when traversing this area.

[[The Old Quarter Ruins]] is a section of The Old Quarter with collapsed docks and building with half hidden alleys.

The [[Sparrow Hawks]] are rumored to operate in The Old Quarter.
