The Argent Spire is one of the sanctioned magic orders of the [[Bentang]] empire. It serves as both enforcers of magical law and expeditionary agents.

The symbol of the Argent Spire is: On a lozenge sable, a tower argent upon a sun in its splendour or. Banners of the Argent Spire have the lozenge set upon white fabric.

There are three different arms of the Argent Spire: [[The Argent Expeditionary]], [[The Argent Inquisition]], and [[The Argent Justiciary]].

The first rank within the Argent Spire is Initiate. Every new member of the Argent Spire starts as an Initiate. Upon completion of their training and passing the promotion exam, an Initiate can be promoted into one of the three arms of the Argent Spire.
