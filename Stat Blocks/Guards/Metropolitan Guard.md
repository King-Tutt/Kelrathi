### Traits
---
***Formation Tactics.*** The guard has Advantage on saving throws against being charmed, frightened, grappled, or restrained while it is within 5 feet of an ally.


### Action
---
***Longsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, Sap, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

***Crossbow, Heavy*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, Push, range 100/400, *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage.

### Bonus Action
---
***Apprehend*** The guard makes a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check (the target chooses which ability to use). If the guard succeeds, the target becomes Grappled.

### Reaction
---
***Reinforcements*** When the guard is hit with an attack, is affected by a condition, is affected by a spell, or a creature it is hostile to takes the Dash or Disengage action, it can use its reaction to call another Metropolitan Guard who shows up in 1d4-1 (min 1) rounds. The Metropolitan Guard can do this a number of times equal to their proficiency bonus. All uses of this ability recharge after completing a Short or Long Rest.
