### Traits
---
***Web Sense.*** While in contact with a web, the spider knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

***Web Walker.*** The spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

***Silk Sense (Patient Predator):*** The spider lays individual silk threads along the ground or floor of its habitat which connect to its main web and extend its Web Sense to the affected area.

### Action
---
***Bite*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage. On a hit, the target must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 3 (1d6) Poison damage on a failed save or taking half as much damage on a successful save. If the Poison damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the target is Stable but has the Poisoned condition for 1 hour, even after regaining Hit Points, and is paralyzed while Poisoned in this way.

***Web Snare*** _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +5 to hit, range 10/50 ft.. A Large or smaller creature hit by Web Snare is Restrained until freed. A creature can use its action to make a DC 13 Strength check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success. Dealing 5 slashing damage to the net (AC 10) also frees the creature without harming it, ending the effect and destroying the net.

### Bonus Action
---
***Cunning Retreat*** The Silkshade moves up to half its movement speed. Moving in such a way does not provoke Opportunity Attacks. The path of travel is covered in sticky webs and is difficult terrain. The sticky webs last until the beginning of the Silkshade Spider's next turn and behave as the Web spell. The save DC for this ability is a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.

### Reaction
---
***Ambush Drop:*** When the spider detects a creature in or on its web or silk threads and that creature is below it, the spider can drop on that creature (do not roll fall damage) and attempt to Grapple it. If the spider successfully grapples the creature, it can climb back up its silk dragline with the creature, using its movement to do so. The spider can only carry creatures of Medium size or smaller in such a way.
