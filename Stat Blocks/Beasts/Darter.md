### Traits
---
***Hold Breath** The darter can hold its breath for up to 15 minutes.

***Water Camouflage*** The darter has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide while completely submerged or swimming with only its head and neck above water.

### Action
---
***Spear Beak*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1 (1d1 + 0) piercing damage. If the target is smaller than the giant darter, it is grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the giant darter can't use its beak attack on another target.
