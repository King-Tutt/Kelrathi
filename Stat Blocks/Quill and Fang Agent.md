### Traits
---
***Fey Ancestry*** The agent has *Advantage* on saving throws against being *Charmed*, and magic can't put the agent to sleep.

### Action
---
***Multiattack*** The agent makes two *Shortsword* attacks.

***Shortsword*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, Vex, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage

***Light Crossbow*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, Slow, range 80/320, *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage

***Tranquilizer Dart (1/Day)*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, range 20/60 ft., *Hit:* 1 Piercing damage. On a successful hit, the targeted creature acts as though it is targeted by the Sleep spell. This effect uses the agent's spell save DC.

***Spellcasting*** the agent's spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). It can cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

1/Day: ***Passwithout Trace***
2/Day: ***Misty Step***
### Bonus Action
---
***Nimble Escape*** The agent can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

***Even the Odds*** If the agent hits with its Light Crossbow, the agent throws a Tranquilizer Dart.
