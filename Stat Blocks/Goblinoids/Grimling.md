### Traits
---
***Ambusher:*** You can set a Snare as the spell. This snare is non magical in nature, and requires at least 20 feet of rope per use. This rope is recoverable if it has not been damaged. You choose Intelligence or Wisdom to determine the save DC of this ability. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus. All uses of this ability are restored when the Goblin completes a Long Rest.

***Fey Ancestry:*** You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

***Fury of the Small*** Once per turn, when the Goblin deals damage to a creature with an attack or a spell and that creature's size is larger than the Goblin's, the Goblin deals extra damage equal to its Proficiency Bonus. The Goblin can use this ability a number of times equal to its Proficiency Bonus. All uses of this ability are restored when the Goblin completes a Long Rest.

***Guerrilla:*** You are proficient with firearms and explosives. You are proficient with your choice of one set of Artisans Tools, Poisoner's Kit, or Thieves' Tools.

***Gun Fighter:*** Being with in 5 feet of a hostile creature does not impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls with firearms.

***Little Bugger*** Goblins are chaotic tricksters and get a +2 bonus to AC.

***Techno-Magic:*** You know the Mending cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the Expeditious Retreat spell. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the Levitate spell. You always have these spells prepared. You can cast each once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
